Caught in Kuipers Belt you have to dodge and weave yourself through the
asteroid field. See how long you can last as your ship speeds faster with 
every second.

Mobile: simply tap the side of the screen you wish the space ship to go. 

Desktop: use [f] for left and [j] for right.

the game ends when you crash and the game will freeze so you can see how
you crashed and the score you recieved.
